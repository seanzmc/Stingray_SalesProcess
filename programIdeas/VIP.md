# **Stingray Premier VIP Membership Program**

## Comprehensive Program Outline

---

## **PROGRAM OVERVIEW**

### Mission Statement

To provide Stingray Chevrolet customers with an elevated ownership experience that builds loyalty, drives repeat business, and positions us as the premier Chevrolet dealer in the region through formalized, exclusive benefits.

### Membership Tiers

### TIER 1: Stingray VIP

- **Eligibility:** All new vehicle purchases from Stingray Chevrolet
- **Duration:** Lifetime of ownership (transferable to next Stingray purchase)
- **Welcome Kit Delivered:** At vehicle delivery

### TIER 2: Stingray Elite

- **Eligibility:**
  - Corvette purchases (any model)
  - Multi-vehicle purchases (2+ vehicles)
  - Vehicles over $60,000 MSRP
  - VIP members who refer 2+ sales
- **Duration:** Lifetime membership (maintains status across future purchases)
- **Welcome Kit Delivered:** Premium package at vehicle delivery

### ALLOCATION MARKUP POLICY DECISION

**Critical Financial Consideration:**

| Scenario                              | Revenue Impact                                    | Member Value                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Charge MSRP on limited vehicles**   | -$5,000 to -$20,000 per vehicle (foregone markup) | Elite members save thousands; massive loyalty driver      |
| **Charge markup on limited vehicles** | +$5,000 to +$20,000 per vehicle                   | Program loses key differentiator; perceived as "fake" VIP |

**Recommendation:**

- **Elite Tier:** Guaranteed MSRP on all vehicles including limited allocations (with anti-flip clause)
- **VIP Tier:** MSRP on standard models; market pricing on limited allocations but priority notification

This preserves margin flexibility while delivering exceptional value to top-tier customers.

## RISK MITIGATION

### Potential Challenges & Solutions

| Risk                                                | Mitigation Strategy                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Over-promising benefits**                         | Clear terms & conditions; set realistic expectations in all materials                  |
| **Staff inconsistency**                             | Regular training; tie compensation to VIP satisfaction scores                          |
| **High utilization of costly benefits**             | Usage limits in fine print (e.g., 1 detail/year, reasonable car wash frequency)        |
| **Member dissatisfaction with allocation priority** | Transparent points system; clear communication about allocation realities              |
| **Vehicle flippers exploiting MSRP pricing**        | Anti-flip clause; require proof of insurance/registration; monitor resale activity     |
| **Program costs exceed projections**                | Quarterly cost reviews; adjust benefits if needed; eliminate low-value/high-cost items |
| **Competitor copies program**                       | Continuous improvement; emotional connection through events/relationships              |
