Rewind Pointer (Undo)

This action rewinds the Round Robin pointer to the previous salesperson. The appointment record will remain on the sheet (no rows are deleted or reassigned).
A reason is required and will be recorded in:
  • RR_AUDIT (as an Undo entry)
  • APPOINTMENTS column H (Notes) for the most recent appointment tied to this Undo
If the pointer changes while this dialog is open, the Undo will be rejected and you should reopen the dialog.
